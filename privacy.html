Privacy Policy

Effective date: July 25, 2026

Raha is designed so that cycle and worship-tracking information remains in the app's private storage on your device.

1. Information stored on your device

Raha may allow you to enter cycle dates, flow information, symptoms, moods, notes, prayer records, fasting records, ghusl status, and app preferences. This information is stored strictly in Raha's private app storage on your device and is not automatically uploaded or synced to Raha-controlled servers, other users, or an external provider.

2. Account sign-in

Raha offers email/password, Google, and Apple sign-in so that you can access the app after changing phones. Supabase Authentication processes the account identity and authentication tokens required for email/password sign-in. Lovable Cloud Auth begins the Google or Apple sign-in flow. These services do not receive your cycle, health, prayer, fasting, or Tasbih records from Raha.

Signing in on a new device lets you access Raha with the same account. It does not transfer tracker history to that device; each device has its own local tracker storage.

3. Local-only features and permissions

Prayer schedules are entered and adjusted by you on your device. Raha does not send your location or schedule to a prayer-time service. The in-app guide provides on-device, rule-based responses; messages are not sent to an AI provider. Raha may request permission to send local notifications, which you may manage in your device settings.

4. Data retention and deletion

Because core tracking data is stored locally, deleting entries, choosing Erase data from this device, or deleting the app may remove that information from your device. Raha does not use cloud backup for tracker records. Because Raha does not back up tracker data, it cannot restore it after erasure.

5. Data sharing

Raha does not sell menstrual, health, prayer, or fasting information. Raha does not use advertising analytics or share such information with advertisers.

6. Security

No storage method is completely secure. Users should protect their device with a passcode, Face ID, or Touch ID and keep their operating system updated.

7. Who Raha is for

Raha is designed for Muslim girls and women who menstruate, from young adulthood onward and throughout adulthood. It is not intended for children below the minimum age at which they can consent to data processing in their location.

8. Medical and religious information

Raha is for personal tracking and educational purposes only. It does not provide medical diagnosis or treatment. Religious information in Raha is educational and does not replace guidance from a qualified scholar.

9. Changes to this policy

We may update this Privacy Policy as Raha changes. The revised version will be posted on this page with a new effective date.

10. Contact

Questions about privacy may be sent to raha.tracker@gmail.com.
