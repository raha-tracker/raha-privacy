Privacy Policy

Effective date: July 25, 2026

Raha is a cycle, prayer, fasting, and wellbeing companion built to keep sensitive tracker records on your device.

Tracker data stays on your device

The information you create in Raha—including your cycle records, symptoms, notes, prayer and fasting logs, missed-prayer records, Tasbih totals, reminders, and manually entered prayer schedule—is stored in Raha's private app storage on your device. Raha does not upload or sync these tracker records to a Raha server, other users, or an external provider.

Clearing the app's storage, uninstalling Raha, or changing devices can remove access to this local data. Because tracker data is not backed up by Raha, it cannot be restored after erasure.

Account sign-in

Raha offers email/password, Google, and Apple sign-in so that you can access the app after changing phones. Supabase Authentication processes the account identity and authentication tokens required for email/password sign-in. Lovable Cloud Auth begins the Google or Apple sign-in flow. These services do not receive your cycle, health, prayer, fasting, or Tasbih records from Raha.

Signing in on a new device lets you access Raha with the same account. It does not transfer tracker history to that device; each device has its own local tracker storage.

Local-only features

  • Prayer schedules are entered and adjusted by you on your device; Raha does not send your location or schedule to a prayer-time service.
  • The in-app guide provides on-device, rule-based responses. Messages are not sent to an AI provider.
  • Raha does not use advertising analytics or cloud backup for tracker records.
  • Choosing to email support opens your own email app. Raha does not automatically submit or deliver support messages.

Your choices

You can sign out in Profile. You can use Erase data from this device in Profile to permanently remove Raha tracker data from the current device. This action cannot be undone.

Children

Raha is designed for Muslim girls and women who menstruate, from young adulthood onward and throughout adulthood. It is not intended for children below the minimum age at which they can consent to data processing in their location.

Changes to this policy

If this policy changes materially, the effective date at the top of this page will be updated.
